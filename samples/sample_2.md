# Find 5 Indian B2B SaaS startups in HR tech and summarize their positioning

## Short answer
Several Indian B2B SaaS startups operate in the HR tech space, including Darwinbox, Keka, HROne, PeopleStrong, and greytHR. These companies provide diverse solutions ranging from enterprise HCM platforms and SMB payroll automation to comprehensive human resources and workforce management software.

## Key findings
- Darwinbox is positioned as an India-built enterprise Human Capital Management (HCM) platform targeting large enterprises and multi-thousand employee organizations with deep module breadth and AI-driven analytics. [S8, S13, S14]
- Keka is positioned as an HR and payroll software solution tailored for small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs), focusing on surface polish, PF and ESI automation, and transparent plan-based pricing. [S8, S13, S14]
- HROne is positioned for mid-to-large businesses ranging from 500 to 5,000 employees, offering a flat PEPM model where subscription meters only activate after a go-live period. [S13]
- PeopleStrong operates as a comprehensive Human Capital Management SaaS platform positioned as one of Asia's leading solutions. [S3]
- greytHR provides a full-suite HRMS platform designed to automate and simplify complex, recurring, and critical human resources functions. [S3]
